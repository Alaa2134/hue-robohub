Useful websites

Websites worth knowingWebsites worth knowing

Sites and tools we use and recommend: circuit simulation, 3D design, AI, robotics and programming — each with what it's for and the first thing to try.

48 sites

  • Wokwi

    Wokwi

    Free plan

    Arduino, ESP32 and Pico simulator in the browser: wire the circuit, write the code and run it without buying a part.

    Try: Open an ESP32 + OLED example and change the code live.

    Open the site
  • Tinkercad

    Tinkercad

    Free

    Autodesk's drag-and-drop Arduino circuits with a breadboard simulator, plus simple 3D design. The best first step.

    Try: In Circuits, blink an LED with an Arduino in five minutes.

    Open the site
  • Falstad Circuit Simulator

    Falstad Circuit Simulator

    Free

    A circuit simulator that animates current flowing through the wires: the clearest way to understand transistors, capacitors and op-amps.

    Try: Open Circuits → Transistors and change the resistor values.

    Open the site
  • EasyEDA

    EasyEDA

    Free

    Schematics and PCB layout in the browser, linked to the LCSC parts library, and you can order the board for a few dollars.

    Try: Design a small sensor board and order it from JLCPCB.

    Open the site
  • KiCad

    KiCad

    Free

    Professional open-source PCB design used by real companies. Learn it if you're going deep into electronics.

    Try: Follow Getting Started and make an LED + resistor board.

    Open the site
  • Octopart

    Octopart

    Free

    A search engine for electronic parts: datasheets, prices and alternatives from every distributor in one place.

    Try: Search L298N and compare it with newer alternatives.

    Open the site
  • Random Nerd Tutorials

    Random Nerd Tutorials

    Free

    Step-by-step ESP32, ESP8266 and Arduino guides with code and wiring for almost any sensor.

    Try: Build the ESP32 web server and switch an LED from your phone.

    Open the site
  • Adafruit Learning System

    Adafruit Learning System

    Free

    Thousands of tidy projects and guides from Adafruit, from soldering to CircuitPython and wearables.

    Try: Read the Guide to Excellent Soldering before your first joint.

    Open the site
  • PlatformIO

    PlatformIO

    Free

    A VS Code extension to program Arduino, ESP32 and STM32 like a pro: libraries, autocomplete and debugging.

    Try: Move one Arduino IDE sketch into PlatformIO.

    Open the site
  • Onshape

    Onshape

    Free for students

    Professional CAD in the browser (even on a phone) where the whole team edits one design together. Free for students.

    Try: Get an Education account and design a small robot chassis as a team.

    Open the site
  • Autodesk Fusion

    Autodesk Fusion

    Free for students

    Mechanical design, assemblies, stress simulation and CAM in one program. Free for students and personal use.

    Try: Design a motor bracket and run a stress study before printing it.

    Open the site
  • FreeCAD

    FreeCAD

    Free

    Fully free open-source CAD with no subscription; works on any computer, offline.

    Try: Use the Part Design workbench to make an enclosure.

    Open the site
  • Printables

    Printables

    Free

    Prusa's huge library of print-ready models: robot parts, tools, enclosures and lab helpers.

    Try: Search “robot arm” and print one in the lab.

    Open the site
  • Thingiverse

    Thingiverse

    Free

    The oldest and largest free library of 3D designs, with a ready part for almost any idea.

    Try: Look for a “servo mount” before designing your own.

    Open the site
  • GrabCAD Library

    GrabCAD Library

    Free

    CAD models of real parts (motors, sensors, boards) to drop into your robot assembly at the right size.

    Try: Download an Arduino Uno model to place your mounting holes.

    Open the site
  • Google Colab

    Google Colab

    Free plan

    Python in the browser with a free GPU: train AI models without a powerful computer.

    Try: Open a notebook and switch the runtime to GPU.

    Open the site
  • Kaggle

    Kaggle

    Free

    Ready datasets, competitions and short ML courses, with other people's solutions to learn from.

    Try: Finish Intro to Machine Learning, then try the Titanic competition.

    Open the site
  • Hugging Face

    Hugging Face

    Free plan

    The home of open models for text, images and audio: try any of them in the browser and use it in a few lines.

    Try: Try an image-captioning Space, then call it from Colab.

    Open the site
  • Teachable Machine

    Teachable Machine

    Free

    Train an image, sound or pose classifier from your webcam in minutes with no code, then export it.

    Try: Teach it three hand gestures and drive a robot with them.

    Open the site
  • Roboflow

    Roboflow

    Free plan

    Computer-vision datasets end to end: labeling, YOLO training and export in one place.

    Try: Shoot 100 photos of a part and train a detector.

    Open the site
  • Edge Impulse

    Edge Impulse

    Free plan

    TinyML: small AI models that run on microcontrollers like the ESP32 or Nano 33 for sound, motion and images.

    Try: Make a keyword spotter that lights an LED.

    Open the site
  • Ultralytics YOLO

    Ultralytics YOLO

    Free

    The official YOLO docs: object detection, tracking and segmentation from a camera in a few lines of Python.

    Try: Run YOLO on your webcam and count people in view.

    Open the site
  • ROS 2 Docs

    ROS 2 Docs

    Free

    The robot operating system used by companies and universities; official tutorials from install to navigation.

    Try: Finish Beginner: CLI tools and drive turtlesim.

    Open the site
  • Webots

    Webots

    Free

    Open-source robot simulator with ready robots, sensors and physics: test your algorithm before building.

    Try: Open the e-puck example and write a Python line follower.

    Open the site
  • Gazebo

    Gazebo

    Free

    The simulator that works with ROS: 3D worlds, LiDAR and cameras for your robot.

    Try: Run a TurtleBot in Gazebo and map a room.

    Open the site
  • MATLAB Onramp

    MATLAB Onramp

    Free

    Free short MathWorks courses with certificates: MATLAB, Simulink, control and signal processing.

    Try: Finish Simulink Onramp and model a DC motor.

    Open the site
  • Node-RED

    Node-RED

    Free

    Wire devices and APIs together with drag and drop: read a sensor, store it, build a dashboard and alerts.

    Try: Run it on a Raspberry Pi and chart a temperature sensor.

    Open the site
  • ThingSpeak

    ThingSpeak

    Free plan

    Send sensor readings from an ESP32 to the cloud and see live charts, with MATLAB analysis.

    Try: Post the temperature every minute and watch it on your phone.

    Open the site
  • Blynk

    Blynk

    Free plan

    Build a phone app that controls your device (buttons, sliders, readings) without writing mobile code.

    Try: Switch a relay from your phone while you're out.

    Open the site
  • Arduino Cloud

    Arduino Cloud

    Free plan

    Online Arduino editor, dashboards and variables synced between the board and the cloud.

    Try: Connect an ESP32 and build a dashboard with a switch and a sensor.

    Open the site
  • GitHub

    GitHub

    Free for students

    Home for your code and team projects, and the portfolio employers look at. The Student Developer Pack unlocks paid tools for free.

    Try: Create a repo for your project with a README, photos and how to run it.

    Open the site
  • roadmap.sh

    roadmap.sh

    Free

    Clear learning maps for every path (backend, AI, embedded, DevOps): what to learn first and next.

    Try: Open your track's roadmap and tick what you've done.

    Open the site
  • freeCodeCamp

    freeCodeCamp

    Free

    Completely free programming courses with certificates: web, Python, data analysis and ML, all hands-on.

    Try: Start Scientific Computing with Python.

    Open the site
  • CS50 (Harvard)

    CS50 (Harvard)

    Free

    The world's best-known intro to computer science, from C to Python, SQL and the web, free with a certificate.

    Try: Watch lecture 0 and solve Problem Set 1.

    Open the site
  • LeetCode

    LeetCode

    Free plan

    Algorithm and data-structure problems asked in company interviews; daily practice makes the difference.

    Try: Solve one Easy problem a day for a month.

    Open the site
  • Exercism

    Exercism

    Free

    Exercises in 70+ languages with volunteer mentors who review your code.

    Try: Pick Python or C, solve five exercises and ask for a review.

    Open the site
  • Hackster.io

    Hackster.io

    Free

    Thousands of complete hardware projects with parts, code and wiring, plus sponsored contests.

    Try: Find an open contest and pitch it to the team.

    Open the site
  • Instructables

    Instructables

    Free

    Step-by-step photo guides for making things: robots, electronics, fabrication and home builds.

    Try: Build an obstacle-avoiding robot from lab parts.

    Open the site
  • Hackaday.io

    Hackaday.io

    Free

    A community documenting ambitious open hardware projects, home of the yearly Hackaday Prize.

    Try: Make a page for a team project and log every stage.

    Open the site
  • MIT OpenCourseWare

    MIT OpenCourseWare

    Free

    Real MIT course materials for free: lectures, exams and projects in robotics, electronics and AI.

    Try: Find Introduction to Robotics and watch the first two lectures.

    Open the site
  • Coursera

    Coursera

    Free plan

    Courses from universities and companies like Stanford and Google; audit for free or apply for financial aid.

    Try: Audit Andrew Ng's Machine Learning Specialization.

    Open the site
  • edX

    edX

    Free plan

    University courses from MIT, Harvard and more in electronics, programming and engineering, mostly free to audit.

    Try: Look up MIT's Circuits and Electronics.

    Open the site
  • Figma

    Figma

    Free for students

    Design app and web screens and posters with your team in real time. Free for students.

    Try: Design your robot's control app before coding it.

    Open the site
  • Canva

    Canva

    Free plan

    Posters, presentations and social posts from templates, even with no design background.

    Try: Make an expo poster for your project in 15 minutes.

    Open the site
  • Notion

    Notion

    Free plan

    One place for project notes, tasks and docs for the team; free for students with a university email.

    Try: Set up a project page with tasks, parts and links.

    Open the site
  • Overleaf

    Overleaf

    Free plan

    Write reports and papers in LaTeX in the browser with your team, using conference and university templates.

    Try: Write your project report in the IEEE template.

    Open the site
  • draw.io

    draw.io

    Free

    Free diagrams (flowcharts, block diagrams, system architecture) with no account.

    Try: Draw your project's block diagram: sensors → controller → motors.

    Open the site
  • Excalidraw

    Excalidraw

    Free

    A hand-drawn style online whiteboard to sketch ideas with your team live.

    Try: Start a shared session and sketch the next project.

    Open the site
