01Vision & mission

Learn. Build. Compete. Lead.Learn. Build. Compete. Lead.

Vision

To be the leading student community at Horus University for robotics and technology — recognised locally and internationally for innovation, impactful projects and student success.

Mission

To empower students through hands-on learning, collaboration and real-world projects in robotics, AI and emerging technologies — preparing them for national and international competitions and future careers.

Who we are

BuildX HUE is a student-led community at Horus University that brings together students who are passionate about robotics, technology, engineering and innovation. We learn together, build real-world projects, and prepare for national and international competitions.

02Why BuildX HUE?

From learning to real impactFrom learning to real impact

Many students love technology and robotics, but run into the same problems:

  • Mostly theory

    Most of what we study is theoretical, with few chances to build.

  • No team to join

    It's hard to find a team and guidance for competitions.

  • Study vs. industry

    Few chances to connect what we learn with real applications.

  • Few competitions

    Many students miss national and international events.

Here's how we help

  • Hands-on projects

    Build real skills by building real things.

  • Strong teams

    Collaborate, learn and grow together.

  • Competition prep

    Represent HUE locally and internationally.

  • Industry connections

    Bridge the gap to real-world opportunities.

04Organizational structure

Students leading studentsStudents leading students

BuildX HUE is student-led, with a clear structure that keeps the community growing, collaborative and sustainable.

Community Lead

Overall leadership, strategy and external relations.

Vice Lead

Supports the lead and oversees daily operations.

  • Technical Team

    Manages all technical tracks and projects.

  • Operations Team

    Organizes events, logistics and resources.

  • HR Team

    Recruitment, member experience and internal activities.

  • PR & Outreach Team

    Communication, marketing and partnerships.

  • Media & Design Team

    Content, visual identity and event coverage.

Members

Active participants in tracks, projects, events and community activities — that's you.

Faculty support

Guided by faculty advisors from the Faculty of Artificial Intelligence, so the community stays aligned with the university's vision and academic values.
