How a robot is born

01

Idea

Every machine starts as a problem worth solving — sketched on a whiteboard, argued over, and turned into requirements.

Problem → requirements

02

Learning

Five tracks and a six-week bootcamp give every member the fundamentals: electronics, firmware, CAD and control.

6-week bootcamp · 5 tracks

03

Engineering

Design reviews, CAD assemblies, schematics and control models — decisions made before a single part is ordered.

CAD · schematics · control

04

Building

Boards get soldered, parts get printed and machined, harnesses get routed. The bill of materials becomes a robot.

PCB · 3D print · assembly

05

Testing

Track replicas, test logs and A/B tuning. We break it on purpose, then remove the failure mode for good.

Test logs · track replicas

06

Competing

Five teams read rulebooks as specifications and arrive with spares, checklists and a robot that already survived worse.

5 teams · rulebook as spec

07

Achieving

Results are logged, lessons are written down — and the next iteration starts the morning after.

Results, logged
